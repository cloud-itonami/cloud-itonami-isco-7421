# Business Model: Electronics Repair Service Coordination Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-7421`
- ISCO-08: `7421`
- Occupation: Electronics Mechanics and Servicers
- Social impact: technician-safety, equipment-reliability, service-continuity

## Customer

- electronics-repair service contractors
- independent electronics mechanic/servicer crews / service cooperatives

## Offer

- technician/service-call shift/task scheduling coordination
- service-call/parts-usage/diagnostic-status record logging
- electronics-parts supply-order coordination
- safety-concern surfacing to a shop safety officer

## Revenue

- monthly retainer
- per-crew coordination fee

## Trust Controls

- no direct finalization of an electronics-repair-execution
  decision (e.g. deciding to proceed with a specific electronics
  repair), ever
- no override or bypass of a shop-safety-officer's judgment, ever
- flagged safety concerns (capacitor-discharge shock risk, solder-fume
  exposure, electrical hazard) always route to human sign-off,
  regardless of confidence
- no supply order above the registered cost threshold without
  governor-gated human sign-off
- operating and coordination records are auditable, not editable
